How lead distribution works

  • Specialty match first — a wedding inquiry goes to an instructor tagged Wedding, if one is active.
  • Least busy wins — among matching instructors, the one with the fewest open leads gets the next one.
  • No specialist? No problem — falls back to the least-busy active instructor studio-wide.
  • Toggle instructors off — inactive instructors get skipped entirely, e.g. while they're out.
